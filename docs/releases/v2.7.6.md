# ARGON 2.7.6

- **Multi-Platform Android Social Media Extraction:** Overhauled pure-Rust extractors for TikTok, YouTube, Twitter / X, Facebook, Reddit, and Pinterest.
- **Audio Filter Fix on Mobile:** Resolved an issue where Android builds discarded video formats and photo carousels lacking separate audio flags. Adaptive video streams and photo slides are now properly retained and resolvable.
- **TikTok High-Speed Dual Engine:** Integrated TikMate direct extraction alongside TikWM and embed scrapers, delivering instantaneous HD and SD video downloads without watermarks.
- **YouTube Cipher & InnerTube Improvements:** Added `signatureCipher` parsing and prioritized the official Android client endpoint, enabling seamless format listing and direct media downloads.
- **Twitter / X Video & Photo Support:** Fixed endpoint pathing to `api.fxtwitter.com/status` and `api.fixupx.com`, adding full support for downloading both high-resolution video variants and photos.
- **Facebook & Reddit Enhancements:** Added mobile fallback scraping for Facebook videos/reels and expanded Reddit extraction to cover image galleries, photo posts, and video fallbacks.
- **Pinterest Integration:** Added pure-Rust extraction for Pinterest video pins and full-resolution pin images.

## Install & Upgrade

- **Windows x64 Installer:** Run **ARGON_2.7.6_x64-setup.exe** (`com.otium.downloader`, currentUser NSIS).
- **Windows x64 Portable:** Extract **argon-2.7.6-portable.zip**.
- **Android arm64:** Install **ARGON_2.7.6_arm64.apk** (`com.otium.downloader`).
- **Browser Extension:** Load **argon-2.7.6-browser-extension.zip**.
